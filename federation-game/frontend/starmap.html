How to Read This Map

What Am I Looking At?

This is a spatial visualization of the Federation simulation — 39 AI characters (NPCs) running autonomously across 8 factions. Each glowing dot is a living character thinking, deciding, and acting every few seconds.

Faction Territory Regions

  • Colored bubbles — Each faction has an influence zone shown as a translucent colored bubble. This is the faction's home sector, where its members operate. It represents influence, not hard ownership.
  • Bubble size — Scales with the number of NPCs in that faction. More members = larger influence area.
  • Bubble border — Dashed when influence is low (cohesion < 40), solid when stable (cohesion 40+).
  • Grey space between bubbles — Neutral / unclaimed space. No faction controls it yet.
  • Overlapping bubbles — Contested or shared influence zones. Two factions are operating in close proximity.

NPC Dots — Who Belongs Where

  • Dot border color — Shows faction affiliation. A dot with a red border belongs to Military Command. A blue border = Research Division, etc.
  • Grey border — Unaffiliated NPC (companion, neutral, enigma, rival, or unknown).
  • Dot fill color — Current mood (see below).
  • Dot size — How recently the NPC was active. Bigger = more recent.

What the Dot Colors Mean (Mood)

  • Positive (happy, inspired)
  • Thinking (contemplative, curious)
  • Uneasy (anxious, suspicious)
  • Negative (frustrated, aggressive)
  • Unknown/Idle

Lines Between Dots

  • Green lines — Positive relationship (trust, alliance). Thicker = stronger.
  • Red lines — Negative relationship (rivalry, hostility). Thicker = more intense.
  • No line — Weak or no significant relationship.

View Modes (Top-Left Buttons)

  • Territory — Emphasizes faction regions, dims relationship lines. Best for understanding who controls what.
  • Network — Emphasizes relationship lines, dims territory fills. Best for seeing who knows whom and how they relate.
  • Crisis — Highlights conflict lines (red), anomaly/low-cohesion zones, and rival/enigma nodes. Best for spotting trouble.

Controls

  • Scroll wheel — Zoom in/out
  • Click + drag on empty space — Pan the map
  • Click a dot — Select an NPC, see details (mood, goal, latest decision, thought)
  • Hover over a faction bubble — See faction info tooltip (name, cohesion, NPC count, status)
  • Double-click a dot — Zoom in and center on that NPC
  • Search box (top-left) — Type an NPC name to find and focus
  • Zoom buttons (bottom center) — + / - / R (reset) / FIT

Right Sidebar — Live Events

A scrolling feed of what's happening right now. Each event shows the character name, action type, and description.

Map Legend (Bottom-Right)

A persistent legend shows faction colors, zone types, and line meanings. Always visible so you never lose context.

What to Watch For

  • All dots in a bubble turning orange/red — Faction in crisis.
  • Red lines between two bubbles — Factions turning hostile.
  • Dots shrinking — NPCs becoming inactive.
  • Many dots pulsing — Cascade event just happened.
  • Bubble border turning dashed — Faction cohesion dropping below 40.
