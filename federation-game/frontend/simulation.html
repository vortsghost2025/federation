How to Read This Page

What Is This?

This is a living simulation — 39 AI-controlled characters (NPCs) organized into 8 factions are running autonomously right now. No player is controlling them. They think, decide, act, and react to each other every few seconds (each cycle is called a tick).

Your job as a viewer is to watch civilization emerge. Factions form alliances, declare rivalries, research technology, pass laws, and respond to world events — all without human input.

Top Bar — World Health

The colored bars across the top show the overall state of the simulated world (0–100 scale):

  • Tension — How much conflict is brewing between factions. High = war is likely. Low = peace.
  • Resources — How abundant food, energy, and materials are. High = prosperity. Low = scarcity and desperation.
  • Threat — External danger level (anomalies, environmental hazards). High = the world itself is hostile.
  • Stability — How well the social order holds. High = institutions function. Low = chaos and power vacuums.
  • Morale — Aggregate mood of all NPCs. High = hope and cooperation. Low = despair and rebellion.
  • Anomaly — How active the mysterious void anomalies are. High = strange phenomena. Low = calm.

Cascade Temperature — A measure of how "contagious" events are. At 92%, when one thing happens, it chains into many others (like dominos). Think of it as the simulation's reactivity gauge.

Left Panel — Factions

Each card is one of the 8 factions. Key info:

  • Power number — How strong the faction is overall (influence + military + economy combined).
  • Cohesion bar — How united the faction's members are. Green = united. Red = fractured, members disagree.
  • Colored dots — The faction's stance toward each other faction: Ally, Neutral, Enemy
  • Action text — The most recent thing this faction did (explore, research, trade, attack, etc.).

Click a faction card to expand it and see: exact stance numbers toward every other faction, and a history of recent actions with their effects (like "+3 influence, -2 treasury").

Faction Tech tab — Shows what technology each faction is currently researching, progress %, and completed technologies.

Center Panel — Event Feed

A live stream of everything happening in the simulation, newest on top. Color-coded by source:

  • System — World-level events (resource shifts, anomaly surges, era changes).
  • Cascade — Chain reactions. One event triggered another, which triggered another. "D2" = depth 2 (second link in the chain). These show the simulation is alive and interconnected.
  • Faction — A faction took a deliberate action (declared a treaty, attacked, researched).
  • Broadcast — An NPC publicly announced something (a belief, a warning, a call to action).

Right Panel — NPCs

Each small card is one of the 39 autonomous characters. Key info:

  • Name — The character's identity.
  • Mood badge — Their current emotional state, color-coded: Positive (inspired, satisfied), Thinking (contemplative, analytical), Uneasy (anxious, suspicious), Negative (frustrated, aggressive)
  • Decision badge — What category of action they last chose (explore, research, trade, fight, etc.).
  • Color dot — Which faction they belong to (matches the faction colors in the left panel).

Click an NPC card to expand it and see: their recent thoughts (what they're thinking about), recent decisions (what they chose to do and why), recent actions (what they actually did), corruption/rumor levels, and their relationships with other NPCs.

NPC Quests tab — Shows quest activity: who accepted, completed, or abandoned quests.

Choices tab — Shows the most common decisions factions are making, ranked by frequency.

Bottom Bar — Era Progress

The simulation progresses through historical eras (like ages of civilization). The bar shows how far through the current era the world has progressed, and any triggers that caused era transitions.

What to Watch For

  • Rising Tension + Falling Stability = A war or revolution is brewing.
  • High Cascade Temperature = Small events spiral into big consequences. The simulation is very "reactive" right now.
  • A faction's cohesion dropping = Its members are turning on each other. It might split.
  • Two factions turning enemy (red dots) = Diplomatic breakdown. Watch for attacks.
  • NPC moods shifting from green to orange/red = Something is making everyone unhappy — probably a resource crisis or threat surge.
  • Lots of cascade events = The world is in a turbulent period. One thing leads to another.
  • Anomaly activity rising = The void is waking up. Expect strange events.
