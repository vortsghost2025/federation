How to Read This Page

What Is This?

This is a living simulation — 39 AI-controlled characters (NPCs) organized into 8 factions are running autonomously right now. No player is controlling them. They think, decide, act, and react to each other every few seconds (each cycle is called a tick).

Your job as a viewer is to watch civilization emerge. Factions form alliances, declare rivalries, research technology, pass laws, and respond to world events — all without human input.

Situation Bar — What Is Happening?

The three cards at the top answer the most important questions:

  • Current Situation — A one-sentence summary of the world state. Read this first.
  • Main Risk — The most dangerous metric right now and what it means.
  • Recommended Watch — What you should monitor next to see how things unfold.

Top Bar — Is The World Healthy?

The colored bars show the overall state of the simulated world (0-100 scale). Each metric now has a severity label so you know at a glance whether a number is good or bad:

  • Tension — Conflict between factions. Labels: SAFE / WATCH / HIGH / SEVERE
  • Resources — Abundance of materials. Labels: SCARCE / LOW / ADEQUATE / ABUNDANT
  • Threat — External danger. Labels: SAFE / WATCH / HIGH / SEVERE
  • Stability — Social order. Labels: FRAGILE / UNSTABLE / STABLE / STRONG
  • Morale — Social confidence. Labels: CRITICAL / WEAK / STABLE / STRONG
  • Anomaly — Void phenomena. Labels: NORMAL / STRANGE / UNSTABLE / REALITY BREACH

Cascade Temperature — How "contagious" events are. Labels: CALM / ACTIVE / HOT / OVERHEATING

Left Panel — Who Has Power?

Each card is one of the 8 factions. Key info:

  • Power number — How strong the faction is overall.
  • Cohesion bar — How united the faction's members are. Green = united, Red = fractured.
  • Colored dots — Stance toward each other faction: Ally, Neutral, Enemy
  • Action text — Most recent thing this faction did.

Click a faction card to expand details. Faction Tech tab shows research progress.

Center Panel — What Just Happened?

Events are now grouped into chains when multiple NPCs react to the same thing. Click a chain to expand individual events. This turns 40+ noisy lines into 3-5 meaningful story objects.

  • System — World-level events (resource shifts, anomaly surges, era changes).
  • Cascade Chain — Grouped chain reactions with count, origin, and dominant tone.
  • Faction — A faction took a deliberate action.
  • Broadcast — An NPC publicly announced something.

Right Panel — What Are Agents Trying To Do?

Each small card is one of the 39 autonomous characters. Quest Health summary shows whether quests are completing, timing out, or thrashing. Below that, the quest feed shows individual activity.

Bottom Bar — What Is Unresolved?

Era progress and pending items — how much backlog pressure the simulation has.

What to Watch For

  • CRITICAL or SEVERE severity labels = Something needs attention now.
  • Rising Tension + Falling Stability = War or revolution is brewing.
  • OVERHEATING cascade temp = Events spiraling out of control.
  • Faction cohesion dropping = Members turning on each other.
  • Quest timeout rate high = NPCs are overwhelmed or under-resourced.
