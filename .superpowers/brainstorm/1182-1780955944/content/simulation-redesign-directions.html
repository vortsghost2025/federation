Which direction should the simulation page take?

Goal: a normal human understands the basics in 10 seconds, then knows where to ask the AI and where to watch NPC communication.

A. Human Briefing Dashboard
ONE SENTENCE: What is happening right now?
Who is active?
What changed?
What matters next?
Live NPC communication feed: who talked to who, plans, decisions
Ask the AI: prefilled questions tied to current tick

A. Human Briefing Dashboard

Recommended. The page opens with a plain-language intelligence brief, then points people to AI questions and NPC communications.

B. NPC Reality Feed
Character list + active speakers
Scene title: The current social drama
Conversation timeline: quotes, alliances, suspicions, plans
Ask AI about selected NPC

B. NPC Reality Feed

Best if the simulation should feel like watching living people. Less direct for world/faction health, stronger for drama and relationships.

C. Analyst Control Room
Morale
Threat
Faction Power
Cognition
Events, NPC logs, decisions, filters
AI analyst panel

C. Analyst Control Room

Best for power users and debugging. It preserves the most data, but may still be too much for a first-time visitor.

My recommendation

Use A as the default page, with B as the main content feed underneath. Keep C behind an Advanced tab so the old telemetry is still available.
